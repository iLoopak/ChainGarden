# Art direction — living reaction chamber

**Nature is the hero. Technology contains and reveals it.** Chain Garden is a premium turn-based puzzle played inside an experimental greenhouse. The board is a tactile cultivation tray; the surrounding interface exists to support the next decision.

## Composition

The 6×6 matrix owns the central play surface. Score, remaining turns and dew sit directly above it. A compact specimen hand sits beside it on desktop and below it in portrait. The opening menu appears inside the real saved/initial matrix; there is no alternate decorative garden or campaign hero. The phase strip and collapsible reaction log explain resolution without becoming a dashboard.

## Palette and materials

Forest charcoal (`#0f1b17`) and moss cavities ground the image. Warm stone-green framing (`#2c3d30`) suggests a worn containment tray, with tiny fasteners and etched coordinates. Text uses pale botanical neutrals, with readable green-gray secondary copy. Curved greenhouse ribs and distant leaf silhouettes are static and low contrast.

Species accents: amber pollen for Sunflower, restrained coral for Rose, orchid spores for Mushroom, cool jade for Cactus, electric moss-lime for Clover, and blue-lilac for Wildflower. Mint energy connects active organisms. Amber identifies forecast/commit consequences. No RGB frames, magenta/cyan wash or hacker panels.

## Living specimens

Preserve the six recognizable silhouettes. Layer stems, shadow/root beds, tissue, internal veins, seed or spore nodes and a small ripe aura. Young plants use the same visual language with smaller buds and unfilled maturity nodes. A mature organism is readable through all three filled nodes, a charged corner mark and its illuminated core. Idle swaying is slight and never implies simulated growth.

## Effects obey the game

Every pulse follows a source/target pair emitted by the engine. Growth updates the actual stage at that frame. Harvesting releases the real specimen silhouette and its points. Mushroom briefly reveals the ability it actually copied; this is an echo, not a new mutation mechanic. Combo counters escalate at real harvest depths. Preview routes are dashed and numbered; committed propagation is solid and sequential.

Glow stays local to ripe cores, selected decisions and triggered events. There are no ambient particle systems pretending a chain is happening. Reduced motion keeps routes, ordered logs and score information while removing travel, bursts, sway and delays.

## Preserved and deferred

The 25-turn puzzle, six species, scoring, seeded RNG, queue safety and saves retain their rules. This pass changes framing and presentation, plus a zero-cost, confirmable forecast. Later work should prioritize playtesting, a short guided opening, audio/haptics and real device accessibility rather than more HUD or simulation systems.
